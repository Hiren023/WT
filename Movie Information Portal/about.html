Our Journey

Key milestones in the CineVault story

Q4 2024
The Idea is Born
Frustrated with scattered movie tracking tools, Alex sketched the first CineVault wireframe on a napkin during a movie marathon.
Q1 2025
Team Assembly
Sarah, James, and Maya joined the mission. The team began designing and developing the platform in earnest.
Q2 2025
Beta Launch
CineVault v1.0 launched with 20 curated movies, 8 genres, and a beautiful dark-themed interface that wowed early users.
Q3 2025
Growing Strong
Community feedback fueled new features including the watchlist, trending section, and detailed movie pages with cast information.
