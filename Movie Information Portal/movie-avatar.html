Synopsis

James Cameron returns to Pandora with the third installment of the Avatar saga. As volcanic activity threatens to destroy the Na'vi homelands, Jake Sully and Neytiri must lead their family and allies on a perilous journey to the Fire Nation — a fierce clan of Na'vi who have adapted to live near Pandora's volcanic regions. When an old enemy returns with devastating new technology, the clans must unite like never before. Avatar: Fire and Ash pushes the boundaries of visual storytelling with groundbreaking VFX and a deeply emotional narrative about resilience, unity, and the bond between nature and its people.
