Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about CineVault

Is CineVault free to use?
Yes! CineVault is completely free. Browse movies, manage your watchlist, and explore genres without any charges.
How are movies selected for the platform?
Our content team hand-curates every movie entry, ensuring only the latest and most noteworthy films from 2025 are featured with accurate information.
Can I suggest a movie to add?
Absolutely! Use the contact form above to send us your movie suggestion. We review every submission and update our library regularly.
Is there a mobile app?
CineVault is a fully responsive web platform — it works perfectly on all mobile browsers. A dedicated app is on our roadmap for 2026.
How often is movie data updated?
We update ratings, box office numbers, and other data weekly to ensure you always have the most current information.
